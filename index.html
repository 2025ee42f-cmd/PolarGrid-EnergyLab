Model assumptions and limits

This comparison uses a shared battery model with charging losses and a fixed capacity based on the coldest forecast hour. It allows surplus renewable energy to charge the battery; the renewable cap applies to direct load supply. The simple rule also uses excess minimum-load generator output to charge the battery when possible. Any remaining excess may be dumped and is logged. Generator ramping, startup costs, battery wear, and forecast uncertainty are not modelled. The planner searches a finite battery-energy grid; this is not a guarantee of a continuous global optimum. Predictions assume the forecast is correct and fuel remains available. The exported record supports checking this simulation, not certifying real station savings. How this is computed: for each of the next 24 hours the engine serves forecast load from renewables first, then battery (down to the safety reserve), then diesel — respecting the generator's minimum loading limit and the renewable penetration cap. Fuel is derived from the genset's linear consumption curve, so the projection is traceable to the hourly dispatch. Generator output above the load may charge the battery or be unused.

System advisories

safety-first

Load forecast

24h ahead
Drivers: base station load, heating demand (temperature-driven), crew occupancy, and scheduled scientific instrument load. Heating dominates in winter.

Renewable supply forecast

24h ahead
Solar = irradiance × array area × efficiency, reduced by snow/ice soiling losses. Wind uses a turbine power curve with cut-in, rated and cut-out speeds — output is zero outside that band, which is why wind can drop to nothing in a storm.

Weather forecast

7 days · exponential smoothing + seasonal baseline
Method: Holt's linear exponential smoothing on recent history, blended with a seasonal baseline derived from the station's latitude so the forecast can't drift away from physically plausible values. Chosen over a neural net because it stays stable on small datasets and every output can be explained.
